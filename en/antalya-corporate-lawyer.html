Corporate Law & Business
Consultancy

Company structuring, contract architecture, and corporate compliance.

Corporate law is a dynamic field that ensures commercial activities are conducted securely on a legal basis, safeguarding the corporate structure and managing risks in advance. At our Antalya-based office, we offer sustainable corporate advocacy and consultancy services to domestic and foreign-capitalized companies across a broad spectrum—ranging from articles of association preparation to general assembly processes, share transfers, and commercial risk analysis.

Key Processes Under Corporate Law

  • Establishment and Articles of Association Architecture: Handling establishment stages of joint-stock and limited liability companies, drafting bespoke articles of association balancing rights and obligations among partners, and managing articles of association amendment procedures.
  • General Assembly and Board of Directors Operations: Execution of company general assembly meetings in compliance with legal procedures, legal auditing of board of directors decisions, release processes, and prevention of potential partnership disputes.
  • Share Transfers and Capital Restructuring: Securing company share transfers, share sales, capital increases or reductions, exercising preemptive rights, and safeguarding investor agreements under legal protection.
  • Corporate Contract Management and Risk Analysis: Drafting, reviewing, and revising commercial, supply, dealership, and service contracts that companies will enter into with third parties against potential legal risks.
  • Company Mergers, Divisions, and Type Conversions: Executing legal procedures for mergers, acquisitions, type conversions (change of corporate form), and liquidation processes undertaken by companies in line with their growth targets.

Why Is Preventive Legal Consultancy Important?

When disputes encountered by companies during their commercial activities are attempted to be resolved through litigation afterwards, they create both time constraints and high costs. Every step taken with a preventive law mindset guarantees the commercial security and continuity of your enterprise.
