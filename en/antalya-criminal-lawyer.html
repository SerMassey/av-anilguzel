Investigation, prosecution, and effective criminal defense.

Criminal law disputes directly concern individuals' most fundamental rights and freedoms, namely personal liberty and security, making them highly critical processes that require rapid intervention. At our Antalya-based office, we provide our clients with professional advocacy and legal defense services at every stage—ranging from statements and interrogations during the investigation phase to prosecution proceedings heard in heavy penal courts—to prevent any loss of rights.

Key Processes Under Criminal Law

  • Investigation and Law Enforcement Statements: Providing legal accompaniment to the client during statement-taking at police or gendarmerie stages and interrogation proceedings before the Office of the Chief Public Prosecutor, and determining the defense strategy.
  • Heavy Penal Court Cases: Preparing comprehensive defense strategies in severe criminal files falling under the jurisdiction of heavy penal courts, such as qualified fraud, drug trafficking, intentional homicide, robbery, and forgery of official documents.
  • Objections to Detention and Protective Measures: Operating appeal paths against unjust detention decisions, requests for the removal of judicial control measures, and tracking compensation lawsuits arising from protective measures.
  • Criminal Court of First Instance Proceedings: Active lawsuit and hearing tracking in Criminal Courts of First Instance for complaint-bound offenses, insult, threat, assault, damage to property, and other offenses against property.
  • Appellate and Supreme Court (Legal Remedy) Processes: Submitting legal remedy applications before the Regional Court of Justice (Appellate Court) and the Court of Cassation against conviction or acquittal decisions rendered by local courts.

Why Should You Seek Professional Support in Criminal Law?

The initial hours and the investigation phase in criminal proceedings are the most critical periods determining the fate of a lawsuit. Verifying the lawfulness of evidence, safeguarding the rights of the suspect or defendant, and formulating an effective defense strategy are of vital importance for the protection of personal liberty.
