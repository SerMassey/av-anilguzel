Real Estate & Lease
Law

Title deed cancellation lawsuits, rental disputes, and property security.

Real estate and lease law carry major economic and legal significance in terms of protecting rights based on immovable property, resolving disputes arising from rental relationships, and ensuring the principle of reliance on the land registry. At our Antalya-based office, we provide our clients with professional legal support across a broad spectrum—ranging from title deed cancellation and registration lawsuits to muris muvazaasi (property smuggling from heirs) disputes, rent determination lawsuits, eviction, and urban transformation processes.

Key Processes Under Real Estate Law

  • Title Deed Cancellation and Registration Lawsuits: Tracking title deed cancellation and registration lawsuits based on unlawful registration cases, unlawful property transfers, fiduciary transaction agreements, and specifically muris muvazaasi (property smuggling from heirs) allegations.
  • Rent Determination and Rent Adaptation Lawsuits: Processes for adapting rental fees according to current economic conditions in long-term lease agreements or filing rent determination lawsuits during statutory periods.
  • Eviction Lawsuits and Contract Terminations: Executing eviction due to personal necessity, the requirement of a new owner, enforcement/lawsuit processes based on written eviction commitments, and eviction lawsuits due to two justified warnings.
  • Partition of Property and Prevention of Interference: Terminating shared ownership through court channels (sale or physical division) and prevention of interference (men-i müdahale) lawsuits against individuals unlawfully interfering with the immovable property.
  • Urban Transformation and Condominium Disputes: Legal resolution of decisions made by the board of flat owners in properties declared as risky structures, contractor agreements, and disputes arising from the Condominium Law.

Why Should You Seek Professional Support in Real Estate and Lease Law?

Since real estate investments and rental relationships involve high-value economic assets, even the slightest procedural error or failure to serve a notice within statutory deadlines can lead to major losses of rights. Processes managed alongside an expert attorney safeguard your property rights in the most secure manner.
