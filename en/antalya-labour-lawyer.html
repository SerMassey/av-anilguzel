Severance pay, reinstatement, and employee-employer disputes.

Labor law is a highly sensitive field regulating working relationships between employees and employers, safeguarding the rights and duties of both parties. At our Antalya-based office, we provide professional legal consultancy and advocacy services to both employees and employers in lawful termination of employment contracts, severance and notice pay claims, overtime and annual leave fees, reinstatement lawsuits, and compensation disputes arising from work accidents.

Key Processes Under Labor Law

  • Severance and Notice Pay Claims: Calculating and recovering severance, notice, and bad-faith compensation through litigation to which employees are entitled in the event of unlawful termination of the employment contract or termination without complying with notice periods.
  • Reinstatement Lawsuits and Mediation Processes: Mandatory mediation applications and tracking reinstatement lawsuits against terminations lacking a valid reason for employees with at least 6 months of seniority in workplaces employing 30 or more workers.
  • Overtime, Weekend Work, and Annual Leave Claims: Collecting unpaid overtime wages, national holiday and general public holiday (UBGT) claims, and unused annual leave fees of employees whose working hours exceed statutory limits.
  • Work Accident and Occupational Disease Compensation: Pecuniary and non-pecuniary compensation claims of the employee or, in case of death, surviving dependents deprived of financial support, resulting from work accidents or occupational diseases occurring during work.
  • Mobbing (Psychological Harassment) and Justified Termination: Executing compensation processes and exercising the employee's right to immediate termination with just cause in cases of systemic psychological harassment (mobbing) in the workplace.

Why Should You Seek Professional Support in Labor Law?

Claims and compensation demands arising from labor law are subject to specific statutory limitation periods and mandatory mediation prerequisites. Managing the processes correctly, conducting witness statements, and performing payroll examinations meticulously are of great importance to prevent losses of rights.
