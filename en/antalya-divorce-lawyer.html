Family & Divorce
Law

Divorce lawsuits, custody, alimony, and property division.

Family law is an extremely sensitive branch of law that regulates intra-family relationships and aims to protect personal rights and, above all, the best interests of children in disputes arising between parties. At our Antalya-based office, we support our clients across all family law processes—ranging from contested and uncontested divorce lawsuits to the liquidation of property regimes, custody and alimony disputes, and protective measures—with principles of confidentiality, diligence, and professional advocacy.

Key Processes Under Family Law

  • Contested and Uncontested Divorce Lawsuits: Uncontested divorce proceedings concluded in a single hearing within the framework of a protocol prepared by the mutual will of the parties, alongside litigation tracking for contested divorces based on grounds such as severe incompatibility, adultery, attempts on life, or criminal offenses.
  • Custody and Establishment of Personal Relations: Custody requests, joint custody evaluations, establishing personal relations with children, and lawsuits to modify custody arrangements in the event of changing circumstances, all keeping the best interests of the children in mind.
  • Alimony Types and Pecuniary/Non-Pecuniary Compensation: Determination, increase, or termination requests for precautionary, maintenance, poverty, and assistance alimonies, alongside compensation processes in favor of the faultless party whose current or expected benefits have been impaired due to the divorce.
  • Property Regime and Liquidation Lawsuits: Legal resolution of disputes regarding property division, participation receivables, contribution receivables, and the determination of personal property, primarily centered around the regime of participation in acquired property.
  • Domestic Violence and Protective Measures: Execution of processes for obtaining restraining orders, protection orders, and emergency measures to protect women and family members under Law No. 6284.

Why Should You Seek Professional Support in Family Law?

Divorce and liquidation processes are complex proceedings that can yield devastating psychological and economic consequences for the parties. Having the process managed by an expert attorney is of paramount importance to prevent loss of rights, properly substantiate alimony and compensation claims, and protect the rights of children.
