Law of Obligations
& Compensation

Pecuniary and non-pecuniary compensation lawsuits and resolution of debt disputes.

The law of obligations is one of the fundamental branches of law governing debt relationships between individuals and institutions, as well as liabilities arising from contracts or torts. At our Antalya-based office, we provide meticulous and comprehensive advocacy services to our clients in matters concerning the compensation of damages caused by torts or breaches of contract, debt and collection processes, lease disputes, and the performance of contractual obligations.

Key Processes Under Law of Obligations & Compensation

  • Pecuniary and Non-Pecuniary Compensation Lawsuits: Executing litigation and tracking procedures to compensate for financial losses and emotional distress caused by reasons such as torts, traffic accidents, work accidents, breaches of contract, or violations of personal rights.
  • Debt and Collection Proceedings: Serving formal notices, initiating enforcement proceedings against defaulting debtors, and meticulously executing legal channels for the prompt collection of receivables.
  • Disputes Arising from Torts: Determining damages and fault regarding property or personal assets of third parties resulting from unlawful actions, and bringing compensation claims before the judiciary.
  • Lease Law and Eviction Lawsuits: Collection of rental receivables, rent adaptation lawsuits, evictions based on eviction commitments, and resolution of residential and roofed workplace lease disputes.
  • Debt Disputes Arising from Contracts: Preventing loss of rights in cases of failure to perform debts on time or as required (default), defective performance, penalty clause disputes, and contract rescission (termination).

Why Should You Seek Professional Support in Law of Obligations Disputes?

Statutes of limitations and preclusive time limits play a vital role in processes for damage compensation or debt collection. Expert legal support prevents loss of rights and ensures that you recover your receivables through the shortest and most secure path.
