Enforcement & Bankruptcy
Law

Debt collection, enforcement proceedings, and financial disputes.

Enforcement and bankruptcy law is a fast-paced and rigorous process that enables creditors to claim their rights through legal channels while allowing debtors to protect their rights within a legal framework. At our Antalya-based office, we provide our clients with professional advocacy support in enforcement law—ranging from the collection of commercial receivables and invoices to non-judgment enforcement proceedings, seizure methods specific to negotiable instruments (checks, promissory notes), and objections removal and debt discharge lawsuits.

Key Processes Under Enforcement Law

  • Judgment and Non-Judgment Enforcement Proceedings: Putting court rulings (judgments) into execution or initiating non-judgment enforcement proceedings for the collection of receivables based on invoices, current accounts, or contracts without a court judgment.
  • Execution Proceedings Specific to Negotiable Instruments (Checks and Promissory Notes): Executing enforcement proceedings that provide the fastest collection path in case of non-payment of commercial instruments such as checks, bills of exchange, and promissory notes.
  • Lawsuits for Cancellation and Removal of Objections: Filing lawsuits for the cancellation of objections in Commercial Courts of First Instance or Civil Courts of First Instance to ensure the continuation of enforcement proceedings when a debtor unjustly and groundlessly objects to the proceedings.
  • Seizure, Asset Research, and Preservation: Identifying the debtor's title deed records, bank accounts, vehicles, and social security records; carrying out sales processes (public auctions) by preserving seized movable and immovable properties.
  • Annulment of Fraudulent Transfers (Actio Pauliana): Securing the cancellation of fraudulent (simulated) immovable and vehicle transfer transactions made by the debtor before or after enforcement proceedings with the intent to evade assets from creditors.

Why Should You Seek Professional Support in Enforcement Law?

Deadlines in enforcement proceedings are strictly limited; missing deadlines to object to payment orders or conducting incomplete asset research can render the collection of receivables impossible. Managing the process meticulously alongside an expert attorney is of critical importance to prevent loss of rights and recover receivables in the shortest possible time.
