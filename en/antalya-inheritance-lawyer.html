Inheritance division, title deed transfer, reduction, and will lawsuits.

Inheritance law is a highly technical branch of law governing the procedures by which a person's estate is distributed among legal heirs or testamentary beneficiaries following their death or disappearance. At our Antalya-based office, we provide professional legal support to our clients across a broad spectrum—ranging from obtaining inheritance certificates (certificates of heirship) to the determination of the estate, partition lawsuits (dissolution of partnership), reduction lawsuits arising from violations of reserved shares, and title deed transfer and will processes.

Key Processes Under Inheritance Law

  • Inheritance Certificates (Certificate of Heirship) and Title Deed Transfers: Issuing certificates of heirship before notary publics or civil courts of peace, and completing transfer procedures of the decedent's immovable properties and vehicles in the name of heirs at land registry offices.
  • Inheritance Division and Dissolution of Partnership (Izale-i Şüyu): Executing lawsuits for the dissolution of partnerships over movable and immovable properties included in the estate (via sale or physical division) in cases where heirs cannot reach an agreement.
  • Reduction (Protection of Reserved Shares) Lawsuits: Lawsuits filed to protect the rights of heirs with reserved shares against gratuitous transfers or testamentary dispositions made by the testator in violation of statutory reserved share rules.
  • Will Drafting and Annulment Lawsuits: Designing official, handwritten, or oral wills in compliance with statutory formal requirements; lawsuits for the annulment of wills containing formal defects, lack of capacity, or defect of consent (error, fraud, duress).
  • Rejection of Inheritance and Management of Estate Debts: Legal tracking of processes where heirs apply to civil courts of peace within the statutory 3-month period to unconditionally reject the inheritance in the event that the decedent's estate is overwhelmed by debts.

Why Should You Seek Professional Support in Inheritance Law?

Inheritance divisions and title deed transfers can turn into major disputes due to a lack of legal knowledge during periods when family relations are most sensitive. Working with an expert attorney is of great importance to prevent missing preclusive time limits, bring muris muvazaasi (property smuggling from heirs) claims to court with accurate evidence, and fully protect estate rights.
