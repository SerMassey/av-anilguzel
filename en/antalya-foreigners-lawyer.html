Foreigners & Citizenship
Law

Residence permits, work permits, citizenship, and deportation processes.

In a globalizing world and in Antalya—a hub of international tourism, trade, and living—the freedom to seek rights, residence, and work processes of foreign natural persons involve highly sensitive and bureaucratic procedures. At our office, we provide comprehensive and result-oriented legal consultancy and advocacy services to foreigners seeking legal right of stay in Turkey, Turkish citizenship application processes, citizenship projects through real estate acquisition, and administrative restriction/deportation decisions.

Key Processes Under Foreigners Law

  • Residence Permit Procedures: Making applications for short-term, family, student, long-term, and tourism residence permits, and tracking rejected applications through administrative and legal remedies.
  • Work Permit Applications: Foreign personnel work permit applications before the Ministry of Labor and Social Security, independent work permits, and processes conducted within the scope of exceptional circumstances.
  • Turkish Citizenship Applications: End-to-end tracking of Turkish citizenship acquisition processes through general means, marriage, and especially exceptional citizenship acquisition through the purchase of real estate valued at USD 400,000 or through investment.
  • Deportation and Restriction Codes (V-Codes / G-Codes): Filing annulment lawsuits in the Administrative Court and requests for stays of execution against deportation decisions issued concerning foreigners; as well as applications for lifting restriction codes (such as Ç-114, Ç-136, G-87, etc.).
  • Real Estate and Corporate Acquisitions by Foreigners: Preparing legal contracts and executing title deed procedures for foreign investors wishing to purchase real estate or establish companies in Turkey.

How Does the Foreigners Law Process Work?

Proceedings before the migration administration, consulates, and ministries can directly lead to loss of rights and deportation risks due to missing documents or untimely applications. Especially in deportation decisions, since statutory deadlines to file lawsuits are very short, managing the process alongside an expert attorney from the very first moment is of vital importance.
