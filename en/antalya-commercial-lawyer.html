Commercial lawsuits, corporate consultancy, and dispute resolution.

Commercial law is one of the most critical branches of law regulating the fields of activity of commercial enterprises and companies with its dynamic structure and rules bound by strict formal requirements. At our Antalya-based office, we offer corporate legal consultancy services to companies operating on a local and national scale, providing our clients with professional advocacy support in resolving disputes arising from partnership relations, tracking commercial receivables, and managing company general assembly processes.

Key Processes Under Commercial Law

  • Corporate Law and General Assembly Procedures: Establishment, articles of association amendments, capital increase/reduction processes of joint-stock and limited liability companies, execution of general assembly meetings, and legal auditing of board of directors decisions.
  • Commercial Disputes and Commercial Court Lawsuits: Lawsuit and defense tracking in Commercial Courts of First Instance for disputes arising from inter-company commercial relations, unfair competition, or goods/services delivery disputes.
  • Negotiable Instruments Law (Checks, Promissory Notes, Bills of Exchange): Lawsuits for receivables arising from checks, promissory notes, and bills of exchange frequently used in commercial life, annulment lawsuits in case of loss of negotiable instruments, and execution proceedings specific to bills of exchange.
  • Company Mergers, Acquisitions, and Type Conversions: Legal architecture of merger, acquisition, type conversion (change of corporate form), and liquidation processes undertaken by companies with the goal of growth, restructuring, or altering their partnership structure.
  • Commercial Receivable and Enforcement Proceeding Processes: Collection of unpaid invoices, current accounts, and contract receivables through legal channels (within the framework of enforcement proceedings and bankruptcy law) to protect the cash flow of commercial enterprises.

Why Should You Seek Professional Support in Commercial Law?

A faulty signature or missed statutory deadlines in commercial life can lead to irremediable financial and legal losses for companies. Preventive legal consultancy services minimize commercial risks, enabling companies to grow securely.
