Contract drafting, review, and legal risk analysis.

Contract law forms the foundation of legal relationships in commercial and daily life, ensuring the protection of parties' rights and obligations to prevent future disputes. At our Antalya-based office, we offer professional advocacy services in drafting customized contracts for the needs of our individual and corporate clients, reviewing existing contracts with legal risk analysis, and resolving compensation/termination disputes arising from contract breaches.

Key Processes Under Contract Law

  • Contract Drafting and Legal Architecture: Drawing up bespoke contracts fully aligned with the true intent of the parties, within the framework of the Turkish Code of Obligations and relevant legislation provisions, complying with mandatory rules that anticipate potential future disputes.
  • Risk Analysis and Contract Review: In-depth examination of draft texts submitted by the opposing party; identifying severe penalty clauses, unfair termination provisions, and hidden risk clauses detrimental to the client, and revising them to favor the client's interests.
  • Commercial and Corporate Contracts: Professionally securing commercial relationships through dealership, distributorship, franchise, non-disclosure agreements (NDA), partnership, and service contracts by taking commercial dynamics into account.
  • Real Estate and Lease Contracts: Creating flawless residential and roofed workplace lease contracts, as well as promise to sell real estate and construction-in-return-for-land agreements, in a manner that protects ownership rights.
  • Contract Breach and Termination Processes: Managing notice procedures in the event of failure to fulfill contract terms, determining justified termination conditions, and resolving ensuing receivable/compensation disputes through judicial channels.

Why Should You Seek Professional Support in Contract Law?

A contract drafted with faulty or ambiguous phrasing can lead to costly litigation processes that take years to resolve in the future. A professional review conducted before signing a contract safeguards your rights from the start and minimizes commercial risks.
