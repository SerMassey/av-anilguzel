Practice Areas

Güzel Law Firm focuses on private law and criminal law, providing consultancy and litigation services across 12 distinct fields of law:

  • Foreigners & Immigration Law: Residence and work permits, Turkish citizenship applications, property acquisition.
  • Real Estate Law: Title deed cancellation and registration, partition of property, rent determination and eviction, unlawful occupation.
  • Contract Law: Drafting commercial contracts, reviewing and revising contracts, notices and protocol processes.
  • Commercial Law: Commercial receivables tracking, unfair competition lawsuits, check and promissory note disputes.
  • Corporate Law: Company establishment, partnership disputes, liquidation, and type changes.
  • Criminal Law: Defense during investigation and prosecution stages, heavy penal and criminal court cases.
  • Obligations & Compensation Law: Pecuniary and non-pecuniary compensation lawsuits, debt and collection proceedings, disputes arising from torts.
  • Labor Law: Severance and notice pay, reinstatement, service determination, overtime claims, mobbing.
  • Enforcement & Bankruptcy Law: Execution proceedings, negotiable instruments, seizure and preservation; check and promissory note tracking.
  • Inheritance Law: Certificate of inheritance, rejection of inheritance, preparation and annulment of wills, determination and partitioning of the estate.
  • Family Law: Uncontested and contested divorce, pecuniary and non-pecuniary compensation, custody and alimony arrangements, liquidation of property regimes.
  • Administrative Law: Annulment and full remedy actions, expropriation, zoning and municipal procedures.
For detailed information regarding each field, you can visit our Practice Areas page.

Service Regions

Our office is located on 100th Year Boulevard in Muratpaşa, Antalya. In addition to Muratpaşa, we provide services to clients from all across Turkey, primarily focusing on districts such as Konyaaltı, Kepez, Döşemealtı, Lara, Aksu, and Serik.

Case proceedings are held at the Antalya Courthouse. For clients from outside Antalya, consultations can be conducted via telephone or video conference, and documents can be transmitted digitally. Consultations are conducted in Turkish and English.

Appointment & Initial Consultation

Consultations are conducted by appointment. To schedule an appointment, you can reach us via phone at (+90) 535 712 25 26, via WhatsApp, or through our Contact page. Bringing documents related to your dispute to the consultation helps accelerate the process: contracts, title deed records, notices, notifications, bank receipts, correspondence, and information regarding any existing lawsuit file.

Attorney fees are determined based on the Minimum Wage Tariff for Attorneys published annually by the Union of Turkish Bar Associations, and a written fee agreement is executed. Fees vary depending on the type, scope, and estimated duration of the work.
