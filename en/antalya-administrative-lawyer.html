Annulment lawsuits, full remedy actions, and judicial review of administrative acts.

Administrative law is a technical branch strictly bound by statutory deadlines, protecting individuals' rights against the actions and operations of the state and public corporate bodies, and aiming to remedy unlawful acts through judicial channels. At our Antalya-based office, we provide our clients with professional legal support across all administrative jurisdiction branches—ranging from the annulment of unlawfully enacted administrative actions to compensation (full remedy) processes for damages arising from administrative actions.

Key Processes Under Administrative Law

  • Annulment Lawsuits and Judicial Review of Administrative Acts: Lawsuits filed to repeal administrative decisions deemed unlawful, such as appointments, dismissals, license revocations, or administrative fines.
  • Full Remedy and Compensation Lawsuits: Processes carried out to compensate for pecuniary and non-pecuniary damages suffered by clients due to unlawful administrative actions, acts, or service faults (administrative negligence).
  • Zoning Plan and Expropriation Disputes: Tracking zoning plan amendments, license revocations, demolition orders, zoning fines, and de facto expropriation (de facto seizure) lawsuits within administrative jurisdiction.
  • Public Tender and Public Contracts Law: Resolution of administrative disputes arising from public tenders, ban decisions from public tenders, termination or cancellation of tenders, and public contracts.
  • Disciplinary Penalties and Public Personnel Lawsuits: Carrying disputes regarding disciplinary penalties, title changes, appointments, and personal rights of public servants to the judiciary.

Why Should You Seek Professional Support in Administrative Proceedings?

Statutes of limitations (preclusive deadlines) to file lawsuits in administrative law are extremely short and strict. Making applications before statutory deadlines expire following the notification of the administrative act and correctly formulating requests for stay of execution are of vital importance to prevent irremediable loss of rights.
